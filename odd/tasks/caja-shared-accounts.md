# Complete shared-table accounts and Caja controls
Objective: Deliver organizer-configured table accounts, individual settlements, and the requested Caja controls while preserving existing user work.
Branch: `feat/caja-shared-accounts` · Delivery: `ask-on-risk` (no push/PR authorized) · Checks: backend `npm test`, backend typecheck/build, frontend build, targeted SQL verification.

## Specs

### S1 — Organizer chooses a mesa account mode
User requirement: “mi idea era que siempre haya un usuario administrador por defecto que puede decidir si solo se pagara a una cuenta o cuenta compartida cosa que deje que otros ususuarios puedan unirse a la mesa con qr y como dije cada uno paga su cuenta”
- The first authenticated participant starting a mesa comanda is its organizer and selects `CUENTA_UNICA` or `POR_CONSUMO` before ordering.
- In `CUENTA_UNICA`, consumption is settled as one account; users do not join as independent accounts. In `POR_CONSUMO`, authenticated customers can join the same active comanda while joining is enabled and each has an account.
- Store the mode on the comanda, not only in browser state. Only the organizer changes its configuration.
- Use “Organizador” in UI to distinguish this customer role from a system administrator. This is a naming assumption based on the agreed behavior.

### S2 — QR orders belong to the authenticated participant
User requirement: “para pedidos qr automaticamente se debe asignar los platos a cada cuenta de usuario”
- The backend derives the participant from the authenticated session and assigns every QR-added detail to that participant; it never trusts a client-supplied payer identity.
- Each participant's consumption is calculated from their active order details. The existing menu/account flow remains usable for `CUENTA_UNICA`.

### S3 — Separate settlements, one fiscal receipt
User-selected options: “Cuentas propias, comprobante único”; “Separados, en distintos momentos”; “Cobrar cada cuenta completa”.
- In `POR_CONSUMO`, each participant settles their full outstanding account in one Caja operation; one operation may use multiple payment methods, but may not leave that participant partially paid or overpaid.
- Participants can settle at different times, including across cash shifts. Persist each payment with the participant, method/reference/bank, timestamp, and the actual `APERTURA_CAJA` that received it. Unpaid participant accounts carry forward when a shift closes.
- Create one sale and one receipt for the mesa exactly once when all participant accounts are settled. Reports distinguish cash collected in each shift from receipts issued at final settlement; never double-count.
- A single-account comanda uses one account and the existing mixed-payment validation.

### S4 — Organizer can control joining/configuration
User requirement: “tambien que pueda cambiar la configuracion que ideas de configuracion recomiendas poner”
- The organizer can open or close QR joining while the comanda is eligible. Closing joining does not remove existing participants.
- The organizer may change account mode while no participant settlement has been recorded. If order details already exist, show the resulting account allocation before confirmation. Once any payment is recorded, lock account-mode changes; keep the join-open setting separately controllable until the first settlement.
- Asking the organizer to approve every order and manually splitting shared dishes were suggestions only and are not in scope. A QR order belongs to its authenticated requester.

### S5 — Cash count and independent closure
User-selected options: “Desglose por denominación”; “Sí, segunda aprobación siempre”.
- Count physical bills/coins by denomination and calculate the declared cash total.
- Preserve the system-expected amount and record the actual count and difference. A non-zero difference requires an explanation.
- Every close is validated by a different authorized user. Do not block close merely because a shared-table account remains unpaid; its already-received payments stay attached to their original shifts.

### S6 — Delivery cash renditions in Caja
User-selected option: “Integrada en Caja”.
- Keep renditions associated with the active cash turn and visible in cash controls/reports, preserving the existing delivery settlement rules and preventing duplicate movement or revenue entries.

### S7 — Movements, turn history, and Z report
User-selected option: “Turnos + reporte Z”.
- Expose the current-turn movements, paginated/filterable turn history, and Z report. Distinguish receipt totals from cash/payment inflows and movements so the same amount is not added twice.

### S8 — Search and exports
User-selected option: “Filtros, PDF y Excel”.
- History/report views filter by date range, cash register, and responsible user where the source data supports it, and provide PDF and Excel exports.

### S9 — Authorization and scope boundaries
User request: “ok aplica las tareas”
- Implement the agreed requirements above, test and document them, and apply only the reviewed new migration to the authorized Supabase project through its tracked migration mechanism.
- Do not replay unrelated pending local migrations, repair pre-existing remote migration-history gaps, change RLS policies, deploy application code, push, or create a PR without separate authorization.

### S10 — Caja opens with initial float and observation
User requirement: “para abrir caja pide abrir con un monto tambien.”
- Preserve the existing required opening amount and add an optional opening observation persisted in `apertura_caja.observacion`.
- Monosucursal is the agreed scope; do not add branch/POS selectors.

### S11 — Participant sees payment confirmation and own receipt in web app
User requirement: “cuando cajero ya haya registrado su pago , notificar al cliente que ya se registro su pago en un modal o algo asi y cerrar su comanda”
- Customer's Mi cuenta view shows only their own active/closed account, dishes and preparation states.
- On detecting the cashier-recorded payment, show an in-app modal with link to the customer's receipt; do not send through WhatsApp or another external channel.
- Customer can see/download/print their receipt within the web app.

### S12 — Electronic receipt representations for participant settlements
User requirement: “Diseña una BOLETA DE VENTA ELECTRÓNICA y FACTURA ELECTRÓNICA en formato PDF A4 profesional.”
- Cashier selects Boleta or Factura for each participant settlement. Factura requires fiscal RUC/company data; phase one uses the approved A4 monochrome print templates and existing internal series/number, with explicit pending/not-integrated SUNAT status. Do not claim a fiscal CPE/SUNAT acceptance or generate fake QR/hash as real.
- PDF phase one is a browser-printable A4 representation (`window.print` / Save as PDF); no server PDF renderer or live SUNAT integration is in this scope.
